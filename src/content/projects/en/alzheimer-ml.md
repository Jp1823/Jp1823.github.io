## Context

Coursework for the Data and Machine Learning course, framed as a Kaggle challenge: analyse correlations in MRI data depending on the patient's stage — **cognitively normal (CN)**, **mild cognitive impairment (MCI)** or **Alzheimer's disease (AD)**.

## Approach

- Exploration and analysis of the provided dataset.
- Preprocessing to extract the information relevant to the problem.
- Design and tuning of several Machine Learning models.
- End goal: a balanced model able to accurately predict the stage of disease progression.
